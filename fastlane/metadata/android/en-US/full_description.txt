Telos Launcher is a search-focused, privacy-first, free and open-source Android launcher and virtual app ecosystem (GPLv3).

Features:
- Search-centric home screen with instant access to apps, contacts, files, bookmarks, web search, and unit conversion.
- Privacy-first virtual app suite (Telos Comms, Network, Sync, Backup, Calculator, Ermis Mail, Notes, Downloads, System).
- Expressive Material 3 UI with dynamic Material You theming and edge-to-edge support.
- Zero telemetry, zero analytics, zero ads.
- Built-in FOSS ecosystem integration.
