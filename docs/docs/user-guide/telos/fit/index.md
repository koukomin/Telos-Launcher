# Fit

Fit shows today’s activity. **Sources** is the hub:

- **Health Connect** is how Telos reads steps, heart rate, sleep, weight, calories and distance.
- **Google Fit / Google Health**, **Garmin Connect**, **Samsung Health** and **Huawei Health** are opened from Fit when they are installed. Turn on Health Connect (or the Health Connect bridge) inside those apps, then tap **Connect** on Health Connect in Fit.

Telos does not log into Garmin, Samsung or Huawei cloud accounts. Those apps already push into Health Connect on current Android. Huawei phones without Health Connect stay on local Fit data until HMS Health Kit is added.

Grant Health Connect permissions the first time. Fit does not upload your health data. The Health Connect privacy/rationale screen for Telos is Fit’s rationale activity (one per APK).

Steps, heart rate, sleep, weight, calories and distance start empty until Health Connect provides them. Nutrition and water start empty until you log them. Fit does not show sample meals or sample step counts.
